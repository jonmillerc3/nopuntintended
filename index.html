04 / THE FINE PRINT

House Rules

Read it now so nobody can pretend they didn't know in Week 12.

Platform

We run on ESPN. If the app crashes during your lineup decisions on Sunday morning, that's between you and ESPN, not the commissioner.

Scoring

Full PPR. Every catch counts, so draft your pass-catching backs accordingly and stop crying about it in October.

Keepers

Each manager can keep up to 3 players from the prior season.

Keeper Cost

A kept player's price is $1 more than what you paid to draft them the year before. Draft a stud cheap, keep him forever, watch everyone else seethe.
