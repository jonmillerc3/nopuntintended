REGULAR SEASON · WEEK 4

Better Lucky Than 1–3

Sloan brings the fireworks. Estes brings a very cooperative opponent. Week 5 puts both unbeaten teams in the same room.

Four Weeks, Two Perfect Records

Sloan scored 205.92. Estes Park scored 109.14. Both are 4-0. Fantasy football remains a deeply unserious accounting system.

Eric takes over first place and the season scoring lead with 647.38 points. Jon stays unbeaten after beating the only opponent who scored less than him besides Manatt.

Estes would have gone 2-7 against the full Week 4 field. Fortunately, the league only asked him to play Cooper. The championship pedigree is real. So was this week’s horseshoe.

Bear Force One rebounded, WE WIN RINGS won again, and ThunderFunk finally got off zero. Sam LaPortaPottys scored 141.58 and still lost. Some managers get schedule luck. Josh gets character development.

Week 4 Matchups

Estes Park Rangers 109.14 · Cooper Dejean Jacket 100.98

An 8.16-point escape. After three weeks of at least 159.40, Estes won with the third-lowest score in the league. Jon is welcome to call this veteran composure. The rest of us are calling it a very fortunate calendar appointment.

Chris Olave led Estes with 19.6, Ashton Jeanty added 18.6 and Jahmyr Gibbs 17.7. Parker Washington and Dalton Kincaid combined for 3.7. Not exactly a parade.

Cooper got 17.0 from George Kittle and 16.2 from Jameson Williams, but Matthew Stafford managed 9.68 and Courtland Sutton 1.4. Kyle Monangai’s 28.0 sat on the bench while Cam Skattebo started for 7.6. A straight swap would have changed the result; hindsight remains undefeated.

Estes is 4-0. Cooper is 1-3. The trophy does not have a column for how politely the schedule treated you.

Final: Estes Park Rangers 109.14, Cooper Dejean Jacket 100.98

Bear Force One 150.72 · Daejon Loves 118.38

Tetairoa McMillan scored 45.2. Sometimes the recap can begin and end with one receiver informing everyone that he has plans.

Bijan Robinson added 27.7, Josh Allen 18.52 and Rhamondre Stevenson 18.4. Bear’s slide from 154.16 to 122.02 to 110.86 ended with a convincing 150.72 and a 32.34-point win.

Dan got 20.48 from Jared Goff, 16.5 from Michael Wilson and 16.3 from James Cook. Travis Kelce supplied 3.5. After back-to-back weekly scoring titles, 118.38 was a less persuasive argument for leaving $37 at the auction.

Daejon Loves still ranks third in season scoring at 566.32. The unused-money joke survives. The scoring streak does not.

Final: Bear Force One 150.72, Daejon Loves 118.38

Sloan 205.92 · Hawkeye Inside Her 118.02

Sloan won by 87.90. That is a matchup margin with its own mailing address.

CeeDee Lamb scored 41.3, Puka Nacua 27.7, Zay Flowers 25.8 and Sam LaPorta 22.4. Those four combined for 117.2—just 0.82 shy of Hawkeye’s entire lineup.

Brock Purdy added 19.62, Chase Brown 19.1 and Jake Bates 18.0. Sloan had no starter below 8.7. Depth was the preseason selling point; this was the product demonstration.

Joe Burrow scored 23.72 and Jonathan Taylor 22.7 for Hawkeye, but Ja’Marr Chase managed 5.7. Josh Downs and Jake Ferguson combined for 8.7. Hawkeye falls to 2-2 after consecutive losses; Eric moves to 4-0 and first place.

Final: Sloan 205.92, Hawkeye Inside Her 118.02

WE WIN RINGS 127.88 · Manatt Mixers 104.12

Lawrence has discovered that winning twice is even more enjoyable than winning once.

Tee Higgins supplied 26.7, Alvin Kamara 22.8 and Quinshon Judkins 21.6. Ka’imi Fairbairn added 16.0. WE WIN RINGS survived Breece Hall’s zero and DJ Moore’s 2.7 to win by 23.76.

Javonte Williams did his part for Manatt with 31.3. Rashee Rice supplied zero, Garrett Wilson 5.7 and the Chiefs defense 1.0. Malik Nabers’ 23.2 and T.J. Hockenson’s 24.9 remained on the bench.

The Mixers have lost three straight. Lawrence is back to .500. The team name is gradually becoming less awkward to say aloud.

Final: WE WIN RINGS 127.88, Manatt Mixers 104.12

ThunderFunk 168.10 · Sam LaPortaPottys 141.58

ThunderFunk’s weekly scores now read 101.46, 123.66, 133.24, 168.10. Four straight improvements, finally accompanied by a win.

Kenneth Walker III led with 30.9, Emanuel Wilson added 27.0, Chuba Hubbard 25.9 and Brock Bowers 20.6. This week Tom actually used the big tight-end score. Organizational learning is beautiful.

Josh got 36.7 from Kyren Williams, 30.8 from Nico Collins and 21.5 from Carnell Tate. Sam LaPortaPottys’ 141.58 was fourth-best in the league—and still a 26.52-point loss.

Saquon Barkley scored 2.0, David Montgomery 4.3 and TreVeyon Henderson 4.2. The winless record survives, but this offense gave Josh something better than another injury report to stare at.

Final: ThunderFunk 168.10, Sam LaPortaPottys 141.58
